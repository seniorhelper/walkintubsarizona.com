What happens next

After you reach out

  1. A specialist calls you back. Usually within one business day. You can also call 1-888-779-2284 and talk now.
  2. We listen first. We ask how you bathe today, who uses the bathroom and what worries you most. Family members are welcome on the call.
  3. We look at your space. From your photos and rough measurements, or at a measurement visit if you would like one.
  4. You get a written quote. The model, the price and the timeline, in writing. Take as long as you need.

What to have ready

  • Your town or ZIP code
  • A few phone photos of the bathroom and the path to it
  • Rough length and width of the current tub or shower
  • Width of the narrowest door on the way in
  • Any mobility needs, such as a walker or wheelchair
  • Whether you are a veteran or an ALTCS member

No tape measure? That is fine. We can start with a conversation.

Our promise

No pressure, start to finish

You do not have to sit through an in-home sales presentation to get a price. Your quote is free, written and itemized, and it does not change because you took time to decide. Compare it with others and talk it over with family.

On the first call we will not ask for a credit card or push you to book a visit. If a walk-in tub is not the right fit, we will say so and explain what might work better, even if that is a simpler fix like grab bars.

Want to prepare first? Try the two-minute Fit Check, read what drives the cost, or see whether VA grants or ALTCS may help. If you have your own contractor, ask about supply-only delivery. More answers are on our FAQ page.

FAQ

Questions Arizona families ask

How soon will someone call me back after I send the form?

A specialist usually calls within one business day. If you need help sooner, call 1-888-779-2284 directly. Tell us the best time to reach you and we will do our best to call then.

Can my son or daughter be on the call with me?

Yes, and we encourage it. Many families join from another city or state. Just tell us who should be on the call and we will set a time that works for everyone.

How should I share photos of my bathroom?

When the specialist calls, they will explain the easiest way to send photos from your phone. Helpful shots are the whole bathroom from the doorway, the tub or shower up close, the faucet wall and the path from the front door.
