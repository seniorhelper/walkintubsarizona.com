Arizona's winter visitors keep a different calendar

Every fall, thousands of people drive or fly south to spend the cooler months in Arizona. Mesa, Apache Junction, Sun City, Green Valley, Lake Havasu City and Yuma all fill up with winter visitors, and the season runs roughly from November to April. In Yuma alone, an Arizona State University study found that winter visitors staying 30 days or more had an economic impact of more than $450 million in the 2017 to 2018 season.

Source: ASU study on Yuma winter visitors, reported by KAWC.

That busy season matters for anyone planning a bathroom change. Many winter visitors decide they want a safer bathroom after a hard fall, a new hip or a doctor's visit, and they decide it at the same time everyone else does. Installers, plumbers and tile crews are busiest in winter. Product shipping can take a few weeks. If you start calling in January, you may not get a tub in until you are almost ready to leave.

The fix is simple: plan on a snowbird calendar, not a calendar from back home.

The big picture

Three ways to time it

Pick the one that fits how long you stay and who will be in the house.

Option 1

Plan in summer, install on arrival

Get your quote by phone and photos in August or September. The tub arrives near the date you return, and the install happens in your first weeks back. You enjoy it all winter.

Option 2

Decide in winter, install before you leave

Use the season to compare options, try the Fit Check and visit with family. Schedule the install for March or April. It is ready and waiting next fall.

Option 3

Supply-only for a part-time home

If you already work with a local licensed contractor, we can ship the tub or shower kit to your Arizona home so your contractor installs it on a schedule that suits you.

From a distance

How to get a quote before you arrive

You do not have to be standing in the bathroom to start. Most of what an installer needs can be gathered with a tape measure and a phone camera. If you are up north, ask a trusted neighbor, your HOA's handyman or a family member in Arizona to help.

Send these and you will usually get a clear written quote, with a note on anything that needs a closer look in person.

Every quote from us is free and written. Nobody will ask you to sign at the kitchen table, and you can take as long as you like to decide. If you want to understand the numbers first, read what drives walk-in tub cost in Arizona.

  • Length, width and height of the current tub or shower
  • A wide photo of the whole bathroom from the doorway
  • Close photos of the faucet wall and the drain
  • Width of every doorway from the front door to the bathroom
  • Any tight turns, steps or narrow halls on the way
  • Location of the water heater and electrical panel
  • Whether the home is a site-built house, manufactured home or condo

Part-time homes

When supply-only makes sense for a winter home

Many winter homes in Arizona are in 55+ communities, RV resorts with park models, or manufactured home parks. These homes often have their own rules, their own preferred trades and tighter spaces. If you already work with a licensed local contractor you trust, supply-only delivery can be the easiest path. We ship the tub or shower to your Arizona address and share the specs your installer needs. You pay less because you hire your own installer.

A few things to check before you choose supply-only:

  • Your contractor holds a current Arizona ROC license for this kind of work. See how to look up a license.
  • Someone can receive the delivery. Tubs are heavy and arrive on a truck, often with curbside delivery.
  • Your HOA or park allows the work and any needed water or electrical changes.
  • Your contractor has seen the product specs and is comfortable with the electrical needs for jets or a heater.

If you would rather have one team handle everything, our factory-certified installers work across Arizona, and many installs are done in as little as one day. Learn more about how installation works.

Avoid delays

What can stretch a snowbird schedule

Most delays are known in advance. Ask about each one when you get your quote.

Product shipping

Tubs and shower kits ship from the factory or warehouse. Order early so delivery lines up with your arrival, not after it.

HOA or park approval

Some 55+ communities and parks want a form or contractor details before work starts. Ask the office what they need and how long it takes.

Electrical work

Jets and heaters may need a dedicated circuit. If your panel is full or far from the bathroom, plan a little extra time.

Hidden surprises

Older homes sometimes have worn subfloor or old valves behind the wall. A good installer flags the risk up front so it does not eat your last week in town.

Before you leave

Closing up the house with a walk-in tub

A walk-in tub sitting unused for six months in Arizona summer heat needs a little care before you go. Standing water evaporates and leaves minerals behind, and seals can dry out in a hot, closed house.

  1. Run a cleaning cycle. Flush the jets with cleaner, then with clean water. Our hard water guide explains the steps.
  2. Drain completely. Let the drain run until the tub is empty, then wipe out any small puddles.
  3. Dry the door seal. Wipe the gasket and frame with a soft cloth.
  4. Leave the door open a crack. This lets air move so the seal does not stick and odors do not build.
  5. Turn off power if your manual says to. Some owners switch off the tub's circuit while away. Ask your installer which is right for your model.
  6. Ask someone to check in. A neighbor who runs the taps now and then can spot a leak early.

Think ahead

Plan for the years, not just the season

Many snowbirds eventually spend more of the year in Arizona, or settle here full time. When you plan a bathroom change, think about the next ten years, not just next winter. Will a walker or wheelchair be part of the picture someday? Would a roll-in shower serve you longer than a tub? The best time to plan is before you need it. The second-best time is late summer, before the season starts.

FAQ

Questions Arizona families ask

Can I get a quote while I am still up north?

Yes. Most quotes can start by phone with a few photos and measurements of the bathroom, sent by you or a neighbor. You can review the written quote from anywhere and decide when you are back.

Is it better to install at the start or the end of the season?

Early in the season means you enjoy the tub all winter, but it is also the busiest time. Installing in March or April, before you leave, often means easier scheduling and the tub is ready for next fall. Either works if you plan ahead.

Does someone need to be home during the install?

Yes, an adult should be there to let the crew in, confirm the plan and see the finished work. If you will be away, a trusted family member or neighbor can stand in, but most people prefer to be present.

What should I do to the tub before I leave for the summer?

Run a cleaning cycle, drain fully, dry the door seal and leave the door slightly open so the gasket can breathe. Water left sitting in Arizona heat can leave mineral scale and odors.
