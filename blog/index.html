Why these guides

Arizona bathrooms come with Arizona questions

Buying a walk-in tub or a roll-in shower is a big decision, and most brochures were written for a generic house somewhere else. Arizona is different. Our water is hard enough to leave scale in jets and valves. In July the cold tap can run warm. Many of our neighbors live here only part of the year. And state rules decide who is allowed to do the work and how programs like ALTCS help pay for it.

These guides answer those local questions in plain words. Each one gives you steps you can follow, a checklist you can print, and links to the public sources behind the facts. They are written for you if you are planning ahead for yourself, and just as much for the adult children and caregivers who are helping a parent stay safely at home.

Read one, read them all, or skip to the topic that worries you most. None of them asks you to buy anything. If a question comes up that a guide does not answer, a real person will talk it through with you by phone, at your pace.

FAQ

Questions Arizona families ask

Who writes these Arizona guides?

Our team at Walk In Tubs Arizona writes them from the questions Arizona callers ask us every week. We cite public sources such as the CDC, the Registrar of Contractors and AHCCCS, so you can check the facts yourself.

Can I print a guide to share with family?

Yes. Every guide is a normal web page, so you can print it from your browser or send the link to a son, daughter or caregiver. Many families read them together before a quote call.

How often are the guides updated?

We review them when rules, grant amounts or programs change, and each guide shows the date it was last updated. If you spot something out of date, call us and we will check it.
