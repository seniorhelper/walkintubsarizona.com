Who we are

The Arizona site of a national bathing company

Walk In Tubs Arizona is part of Aging Safely Baths, a company based in Denver, Colorado. We do one thing: help people bathe safely at home. That means walk-in tubs, roll-in and barrier-free showers, and the advice to choose between them.

We supply and install in all 50 states, and we ship supply-only to Canada. This site focuses on Arizona because Arizona homes have their own needs: hard water, summer heat, a lot of slab floors, winter visitors and long distances between towns.

Our focus is narrow on purpose. We are not a general remodeler that also does tubs. Every conversation we have is about how someone gets in and out of the bath, how they sit and stand, who helps them and what their bathroom can hold. That focus is what lets us give a straight answer, including the answer that a simple grab bar or a shower chair might be enough for now.

We do not have an Arizona showroom, and we will not pretend to. What we have is a team that answers the phone, factory-certified installers who travel the state and a process that works from Yuma to Show Low.

How we work

No pressure is not a slogan here

Many people call us after a long in-home sales visit that left them tired and unsure. We built our process to be the opposite.

Start by phone

You can talk to us, send photos and get a written quote without anyone coming to your home. A measurement visit is there if you want one.

Written and itemized

Your quote lists the product, the installation and any extras line by line. The price does not change because you took a week to think.

Your pace

Take your time, ask your family, compare other companies. Calling us never obligates you to anything.

You can read more about why we take a different, no-pressure approach on our main site.

Two ways to buy

Installed for you, or shipped to your door

Most families choose full installation. Our installers remove the old tub or shower, set the new one, connect the plumbing and clean up. Many installs finish in as little as one day once the product arrives.

Some people already have a contractor they trust. For them, supply-only delivery ships the product to the home and we share the specs the installer needs. Supply-only costs less because you hire your own installer.

Prices vary a lot by model, features and how much bathroom work your home needs. Your free written quote shows every cost up front.

Plain promises

What we will not do

  • We will not pressure you to sign during a visit or a call.
  • We will not offer "today only" prices, fake discounts or "was/now" deals.
  • We will not promise a tub will cure or treat a health condition. Warm water and jets feel soothing to many people, but ask your doctor about heat, blood pressure and circulation.
  • We will not tell you Medicare will pay. Original Medicare generally does not cover walk-in tubs or shower conversions.
  • We will not make guarantees we cannot keep, or claim to be the "best" or the "cheapest".
  • We will not sell you a tub when a shower would serve you better.

FAQ

Questions Arizona families ask

Is Walk In Tubs Arizona a local Arizona company?

We are the Arizona site of Aging Safely Baths, which is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. We do not have an Arizona showroom. Our factory-certified installers work throughout Arizona, and we also ship supply-only to Arizona homes for your own contractor.

Which walk-in tub and shower brands do you carry?

We carry Ella's Bubbles and Hydro Dimensions walk-in tubs, plus Bestbath showers, which are made in the USA, and ANZZI showers. Carrying several brands lets us match the product to your body and bathroom instead of steering you to one line.

Why are there no sales or limited-time discounts on this site?

Because a fair price should not depend on how fast you sign. Every quote is written, lists what is included and stays the same whether you decide today or next month. That lets you compare calmly and talk it over with family.

How do I check that an installer is licensed in Arizona?

Arizona requires a Registrar of Contractors license for nearly every tub or shower installation. Ask any installer, including ours, for the license number and look it up at roc.az.gov. It takes about a minute.
