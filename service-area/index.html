Rural Arizona

How scheduling and delivery work outside the big cities

Arizona is a big state. A home in Springerville, Bisbee or Page can be many hours from the nearest large warehouse. That does not mean you wait at the back of the line. It means we plan a little differently.

We group installs by region, so a crew heading to the White Mountains or southeastern Arizona can serve several homes on one trip. Before we order, we confirm whether a freight truck can reach your house, whether there are gates, dirt roads or steep driveways, and who will be home on delivery day.

Once the product arrives, many installs take as little as one day. If your home needs extra plumbing or wall work, we say so in the written quote, so you are not surprised by a second trip.

Weather matters too. Monsoon storms in summer and snow in the high country can close roads or make carrying a heavy tub unsafe. If that happens, we call you, explain the change and pick a new date together.

Already work with a trusted local contractor? Supply-only delivery sends the tub or shower kit straight to your home. In Arizona, nearly every tub or shower job needs a contractor licensed by the Registrar of Contractors, so ask for the license number and look it up at roc.az.gov.

  1. Call or send photos. Tell us your town and how you bathe today.
  2. Remote measuring. Rough measurements and photos let us narrow the choices without a long drive.
  3. Written quote. Model, price, route and timeline, all in writing.
  4. Regional install or delivery. Scheduled with other homes in your area when possible.

Local help

Help close to home, in every county

Arizona's Area Agencies on Aging cover the whole state by region. They can point you to caregiver support, meal and transportation programs, and in some areas home repair or barrier-removal help for qualifying adults 60 and older. Funds are limited and often have waitlists, so it pays to call early, even before you choose a product.

In Maricopa County, the Region One Senior HelpLine is 602-264-4357. In Pima County, the Pima Council on Aging is 520-790-7262. Everywhere else, the Arizona DES directory lists the agency for your county.

Veterans can ask the Phoenix, Tucson or Prescott VA medical centers, or a nearby VA clinic, about the HISA grant for bathroom changes. ALTCS members can ask their case manager about a roll-in shower.

Questions to ask your local agency

  • Do you offer home repair or barrier-removal help?
  • Who qualifies, and is there a waitlist?
  • Do you need a written contractor quote?
  • Can a family member apply for me?

FAQ

Questions Arizona families ask

Is my small Arizona town too far away for installation?

No. We install anywhere in Arizona, including small towns and rural addresses far from Phoenix and Tucson. Remote jobs take more planning, so we confirm the route, the install date and anything unusual about the property in your written quote.

How long does it take to get a walk-in tub to a rural Arizona address?

It depends on the model you choose and where you live. Some products ship quickly and others are made to order, and rural installs are grouped by region. Your written quote gives you a realistic timeline before you commit.

Can my local plumber in Show Low, Safford or Nogales install a tub you ship?

Yes. Supply-only delivery sends the tub or shower kit to your home for your own contractor, and we share the specs and rough-in details your installer needs. In Arizona that contractor should hold an ROC license for nearly every tub or shower job.

How do I find the Area Agency on Aging for my Arizona county?

Arizona DES keeps a directory of Area Agencies on Aging by region. These agencies can point you to caregiver support and, in some areas, home repair or barrier-removal programs for qualifying adults 60 and older. Funds are limited, so call early.

I spend part of the year in another state. Can you help there too?

Yes. Aging Safely Baths supplies and installs walk-in tubs and roll-in showers in all 50 states and offers supply-only delivery to Canada. If you have a home in Arizona and another one elsewhere, one call covers both.
