Your privacy, in plain words

What we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

Last updated: October 8, 2026.

This policy covers walkintubsarizona.com, run by Walk In Tubs Arizona, an Aging Safely Baths company headquartered in Denver, Colorado. We wrote it to be short and readable. If anything here is unclear, call us at 1-888-779-2284.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us. When you fill out our quote or contact form, it can include:

  • Your name, phone number, ZIP code and email address
  • What you are interested in, such as a walk-in tub or a roll-in shower
  • Whether you want installation or supply-only delivery
  • Your timeline and how you plan to pay, such as a VA grant
  • Any message you write to us
  • Your consent to be contacted by phone or text

If you call us, we may take notes about your questions and your bathroom so we can prepare a quote.

How the form works

When you press send, the form is delivered to our team by email through a service called FormSubmit. FormSubmit passes your message along to us. We do not store form entries in a public database on this website.

How we use your information

We use what you send only to respond to you, answer your questions and prepare your quote. That can include calling or texting you about your request, and sharing the details our installers or product suppliers need to measure, quote, deliver or install your tub or shower.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information
  • We do not rent or trade it to marketing lists
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site

Calls and texts

If you agree to be contacted by phone or text, you can change your mind at any time. Just tell us on a call, reply to a text asking us to stop, or call 1-888-779-2284 and ask. We will stop contacting you about your request.

Outside services this site uses

To display the site, a few items load from other companies' servers. Our fonts come from Google Fonts, and the Better Business Bureau seal loads from the BBB. When your browser loads these, those services may receive basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

Keeping your information safe

We limit access to your information to the people who need it to help you. No system is perfect, so please do not send financial account numbers, Social Security numbers or medical records through the form.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it for a job in progress or for legal reasons. Call 1-888-779-2284 to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. You can always contact us or return to the home page.
